Counting rules

  • The corpus: every INSPIRE-HEP literature record with 1 to 10 authors (ac 1->10).
  • A country's papers have an author affiliated to one of its institutions (the country of the institution's first address in INSPIRE); each paper counts once per country.
  • A country's authors are the distinct authors (profile, else INSPIRE BAI, else name) with such an affiliation.
  • Citations are INSPIRE's citation count, from all of INSPIRE; citations from the corpus come from the references of the corpus. The h-index uses INSPIRE's count.
  • References: only those INSPIRE links to a record. Each is flagged in_corpus when it points to a paper of the corpus.
  • Per million inhabitants: the World Bank population of the latest year, for countries of 1 million inhabitants or more; R&D: the World Bank average expenditure in % of GDP over the years with a value from 1996 on (reproduce/world_bank.csv, reproduce/world_bank.py).
  • Ranks and mobility: a position counts when its author published a paper affiliated to the position's institution in one of its years (start to end, or to now when current).
  • Gender: inferred from the first names with genderize-offline (World Gender Name Dictionary 2.0), with the country of the author's first affiliation; counted at probability 0.9 or more. These are estimates, not self-reported gender.

Run it yourself

The scripts are in restrepo/inspireauth10. The download takes several hours.

pip install -r reproduce/requirements.txt
python build/build_inspire10.py --out data/
python reproduce/indicators.py data/ out/dash/
python reproduce/standalone.py out/dash/ out/board.html --set DATE=YYYY-MM-DD
