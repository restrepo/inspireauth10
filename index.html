HECAP worldwide boards

Each board shows indicators of high-energy physics, cosmology and astroparticle physics for every country, from one download of all INSPIRE-HEP papers with up to 10 authors and their references (repository restrepo/inspireauth10). It extends the study of Latin America by Morales-Alvarado, Quevedo, Ramos-Hamud and Restrepo (its boards). A new board is built each time a release of the repository is published. Every board is a self-contained page that also works offline.
