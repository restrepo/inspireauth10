Where the topics of 2010 went

The topics were computed twice for the papers published up to 2010: once with only the citations known in 2010 (300 topics), and once inside today's clustering of all the papers up to 2026 (304 topics). Each band below is a group of papers that sat in one topic in 2010 and sits in another today. Each 2010 topic is named from its own most cited papers, arXiv categories and years; the 92 that are the same group of papers as a topic of today keep that topic's name.

What happened to the 300 topics of 2010

A 2010 topic is the same topic today when 60% of its papers are in one topic of today and they are 60% of that topic's papers up to 2010 (the rule used to keep the names). absorbed: 60% of its papers are in one topic of today that also took in other topics. split: two or more topics of today hold at least 15% each. scattered: spread more thinly.
